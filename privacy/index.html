Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 Nov 2026

This is the portfolio website of Anish Kumar (HEYYANIXX), Dumka, Jharkhand, India. This page explains what the site collects. Short answer: almost nothing.

What this site collects

  • No sign-up, no account, no analytics and no tracking or advertising cookies.
  • The "start a project" form does not send anything to a server. It only opens your own email app with your answers filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.
  • Your browser saves two small settings on your own device (sound on/off and whether you have seen the tip). They never leave your device.
  • Like any website, the web host may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

When you contact me

If you email me or message me on Instagram, I get what you send (your name, contact details and project details). I use it only to reply and to work on your project. I never sell it or share it, except with services needed to do the work you asked for.

Your choice

Email me anytime to see, correct or delete what you sent.
